How GigFlow Works

Whether you're hiring or freelancing, getting started takes just a few steps.

🧑‍💼

For Clients

1

Post a gig

Describe what you need done, set a budget, and publish it in minutes.

2

Review proposals

Freelancers apply with their rate, timeline, and portfolio. Compare and shortlist.

3

Hire & chat

Message your chosen freelancer directly and agree on the details.

4

Pay securely

Funds are held safely and released to the freelancer once you approve the work.

🧑‍💻

For Freelancers

1

Create your profile

Showcase your skills, past work, and set your starting price.

2

Apply to gigs

Browse open gigs that match your skills and send a proposal.

3

Deliver the work

Communicate with your client and submit the completed gig.

4

Get paid

Once approved, payment is released straight to your GigFlow wallet.
