About GigFlow

Built in Nigeria, for Nigerian freelancers and the people who hire them.

Our Story

Work shouldn't be this hard to find.

GigFlow started with a simple observation: Nigeria has no shortage of skilled developers, designers, writers, and virtual assistants — but finding real, paying gigs (or reliable, verified talent) was still too slow and too scattered across DMs, WhatsApp groups, and word of mouth. We built GigFlow to fix that: one place where talent and opportunity can actually find each other.

🎯

Our Mission

GigFlow was created by Muktar Idris as a web development project to showcase my skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My goal was to build a clean, user-friendly platform that connects freelancers with clients while solving real-world problems.

🤝

Trust First

Every freelancer profile is reviewed, every payment is held securely until work is approved, and every review is from a verified gig.

🇳🇬

Built for Nigeria

Naira pricing, local payment rails, and categories shaped around the work Nigerian freelancers actually do every day.
